In build Project 01

AI Lead Triage for Zoho CRM

Small teams lose leads by answering late or answering the wrong ones first. This flow sends each new lead to Claude, writes the answer back into Zoho CRM, and routes it one of four ways.

  • Reads the customer’s own words, not just form dropdowns
  • Scores intent and urgency, flags emergencies, writes a two-line summary
  • Emergency and Hot leads get a CRM task and an alert to the owner
  • Anything uncertain goes to Manual review instead of being guessed

Tested Project 03

Duplicate-safe lead intake

Automations fail quietly, and a failed run can mean a lost customer. This intake flow checks for duplicates before creating a lead. If it cannot create the lead, it opens an ALERT task in Zoho CRM with the submitted email, so a person follows up by hand.
