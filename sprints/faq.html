FAQ

Everything you'd ask before paying us.

If something isn't here, email hello@mnkiswan.com. We answer within four hours.

What exactly is a sprint?

A sprint is one week of focused work on one clearly defined assumption. You leave with either a working demo you can show people or a written decision memo that tells you whether to build, pivot, or kill the idea. It is not a full product. It is proof, or the absence of it.

What if my idea turns out to be bad?

That's a successful sprint. A Kill Sprint that saves you six months and several lakh rupees of build cost is worth the fee. We deliver the memo either way. The decision memo is the deliverable, not a consolation prize.

Do I own the work you build?

Yes. Full IP transfer on payment. Everything we build — code, designs, copy, interview notes — becomes yours. We keep only the right to describe the engagement in an anonymized case study, and only if you agree.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes, we'll sign a standard mutual NDA before the kickoff call. We won't sign anything that prevents us from describing the general shape of the work in a case study, but we'll happily anonymize names, numbers, and details.

How do I pay?

50% on Monday kickoff, 50% on Friday delivery before the handover. We accept UPI, bank transfer, and international payments via Wise or PayPal. Invoices are issued for every payment.

What if I need more than a week?

Then you book a second week — the Build & Handoff — to productionize the validated demo. We don't stretch one sprint into six weeks. If you need ongoing work, the Weekly Partner retainer is the right fit, and it's capped at two clients so we can actually deliver.

What tools do you use?

Whatever gets to the answer fastest. Typically: AI-assisted coding (Cursor, Claude), no-code automation (Make, Zapier, n8n), landing pages and prototypes (Framer, Webflow, plain HTML), and whatever the client's existing stack requires. We don't have a house stack — we have a house bias toward speed.

What's your refund policy?

No refunds after the Monday kickoff. The brief fee is non-refundable. If we fail to deliver the agreed sprint output, we'll refund the final 50% and you keep whatever was built. This is in the contract, not just on this page.

What kinds of ideas do you accept?

Web apps, internal tools, landing pages, automations, content systems, small games, and business-model tests. We don't take on ideas that require large upfront capital, physical manufacturing, or regulated industries (health, finance, legal) without a specialist partner.
