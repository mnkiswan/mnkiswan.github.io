Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

1. Who we are

MNKISWAN ("we", "us", "our") is a two-person sprint studio operating from India and Poland. You can reach us at hello@mnkiswan.com.

2. What we collect

When you submit the application form, we collect your name, email address, business name (optional), the idea description, your preferred sprint type, budget range. We do not collect payment information on this website.

Our host processes technical information to deliver the site. If you allow ad measurement, Meta receives page views and a conversion event after a successful application.

3. How we use it

  • To reply to your application and scope a sprint
  • To send invoices and project updates if we work together
  • To measure which ads and pages bring in applications
  • To improve the site and our offers

We do not sell your data. We do not share your idea with anyone outside the two of us without your permission.

4. Cookies and tracking

Optional Meta tracking stays off until you allow ad measurement. Use the tracking settings at the foot of any page to change your choice.

5. Third parties

  • WordPress.com — hosts the current application form. Formspree will process applications submitted through the direct form when enabled.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website
  • Meta (Facebook) — advertising and pixel tracking

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy. We choose providers with reasonable data protection standards.

6. How long we keep data

Applications that do not proceed to a sprint are deleted after 12 months. Client project data is retained for 3 years for tax and legal reasons, then deleted.

7. Your rights

Under India's DPDP Act and the EU GDPR (where applicable), you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Use hello@mnkiswan.com and include "Data request" and we'll respond within 30 days.

8. Security

We use HTTPS on this site and store client data in password-protected, access-controlled tools. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps and will notify you within 72 hours if a breach affects your data.

9. Children

This site is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be announced on the homepage.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@mnkiswan.com.
