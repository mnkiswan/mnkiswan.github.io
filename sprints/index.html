How it works

Your five-day sprint

  1. Monday — Kickoff. We lock one assumption and define what "done" means. You get a written scope before we start.
  2. Tuesday — Build. We build the smallest thing that tests the assumption. You review the first version on Wednesday.
  3. Wednesday — Check-in. A one-paragraph update. No surprises on Friday.
  4. Thursday — Synthesize. We turn the evidence into a demo you can show or a memo you can act on.
  5. Friday — Delivery. A 60-minute walkthrough. You keep everything we built. The final balance is due on handover.
